How It Works
1
Live Price Feed
We pull real-time XAU/USD spot prices from TwelveData API, refreshed every 60 seconds so you always see the latest market data.
2
ATR Volatility Model
Our forecast engine uses ATR × √(t/1440), the financial square-root-of-time formula to predict realistic price ranges across multiple timeframes.
3
Market Session Awareness
The model adjusts for Asian, London, and New York trading sessions, peak overlap hours get a higher volatility multiplier for more accurate short-term bands.
4
Momentum & Reversion
Directional drift is weighted by today's momentum score and dampened by mean-reversion pressure, so big moves attract proportional counter-trend probability.
